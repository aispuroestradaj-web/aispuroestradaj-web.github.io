About Me

Hello, and thanks for stopping by my site! My goal is to share my interest and show the work I am doing in the education space. I have been an educator for many years, and I hope to merge education and technology to help teachers and students.

I am currently taking a gap year and will be continuing my master's and bachelor's degrees at Stanford University. I have been fortunate enough to travel through the different study abroad programs in Kyoto, Japan, and Florence, Italy. I hope you will enjoy the photos I have taken throughout the years, and I will continue to post whenever life becomes interesting!

This site was able to come to fruition through the help of Google’s AI, Gemini. I do not claim to be a web developer, but AI has been a great resource to help me set up this website and be able to show my work.
